# Teacher's Day preparation

Gather this before you replace the sample class in the site.

## For each teacher

- The name they will type
- A password you can tell them in person, on the day
- Their subject: chemistry, physics, mathematics, biology, English, or Islamiat and psychology

## For each student

- The name to show
- One sentence for each teacher who should receive it
- A photo only if they, and their family, have said yes. The website is public.

## Lines worth collecting

- A practical that went wrong and still mattered
- A sentence the teacher used more than once
- Something the class never said out loud
- A joke that still belongs to that room
- What they want the teacher to know on the day

Put the finished words in `lib/teachers.ts`. Put permitted photos in `public/memories/`.
